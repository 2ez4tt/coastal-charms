    Everything here started on a beach.

    The shells, the sea glass, the worn-smooth pieces that wash up along the Gwadar coast — they are collected by hand, sorted, and strung one at a time. No two pieces come out identical, because no two shells are.

    If you want something in a particular colour, or a length that fits you properly, say so in the message. Most pieces can be made your way.
